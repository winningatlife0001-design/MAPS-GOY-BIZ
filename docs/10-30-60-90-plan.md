# 10 — The 30 / 60 / 90-Day Plan 🗓️

A concrete path from zero to a real, paying business. The whole thing is designed so **[girlfriend] sells + relates, Claude produces.** Keep active clients to what one person can comfortably serve.

---

## Before Day 1 — Foundations (a few hours)
- [ ] Read `00`, `01`, `07` (legal), `08` (sales).
- [ ] Register as a **sole proprietor** (ทะเบียนพาณิชย์) and open a **business bank account** + **PromptPay**. (`07`)
- [ ] Create your **LINE Official Account** + a QR code for your one-pager. (`08`)
- [ ] Pick **ONE town + ONE segment** to dominate first (e.g. *guesthouses in Ao Nang*). (`01`)
- [ ] Decide your packages/prices (use the defaults in `05`).

---

## Days 1–30 — Proof (get your first 1–2 clients)
**Goal: 1–2 paying builds + your first reference site.**

Week 1 — *Set up the machine*
- [ ] Claude runs the **lead scraper** for your town/segment → ranked spreadsheet with phones/emails. (`workflows/leadgen-apify.md`)
- [ ] Claude builds **one demo site for a real local hotel** (unpaid, as a showcase) — so you have something concrete to show. Use it in every pitch.
- [ ] Prepare your **free-audit** opener + the Thai/English outreach templates. (`templates/outreach/`)

Weeks 2–4 — *Start selling*
- [ ] Contact **5–10 of the 🔥 top leads per day** (Facebook DM / phone → LINE). Always lead with a **personal 2-min audit video** of *their* Google listing.
- [ ] Book calls/visits; use the proposal template; **50% deposit to start.**
- [ ] Deliver the first 1–2 sites (`workflows/build-a-site-sop.md`) + fix their Google Maps.
- [ ] Get a **testimonial + before/after screenshots** the moment they're happy.

*30-day target: 1–2 builds (≈฿25–50k), 1–2 clients on a monthly plan, 1 glowing testimonial.*

---

## Days 31–60 — Repeat & systematise
**Goal: 4–6 total clients; tighten the assembly line.**
- [ ] Use the testimonial + local result as your main pitch ("a guesthouse near you got this").
- [ ] **Ask every happy client for a referral** (warm intros convert best here).
- [ ] Contact 5–10 new leads/day; expand to a 2nd nearby town or the **spa/restaurant** segment.
- [ ] Turn each finished site into a **mini-template** so builds get faster.
- [ ] Start the **monthly Maps reports** for existing clients (the anti-churn habit).
- [ ] Add **Local Falcon** (~$25/mo) once ~5 clients, so reports look sharp.

*60-day target: 4–6 clients, ~฿15–25k/mo recurring building, a repeatable build in <3 days.*

---

## Days 61–90 — Grow the recurring base
**Goal: ~10 clients; recurring revenue becomes real.**
- [ ] Push **"Care + Maps ฿3,900/mo"** as the default — recurring is the business.
- [ ] Lean on referrals + local owner Facebook groups + tourism associations.
- [ ] Introduce the **200% tax-deduction angle** for company-registered hotels (deadline Dec 2027 = honest urgency). (`01`)
- [ ] Upsell existing clients: extra language, photo pack, Google free-booking-links setup, seasonal promo pages.
- [ ] If demand exceeds your time, hire a **Thai VA** for outreach/admin (keep selling + relationships yourself). (Legal note: hiring a Thai employee triggers Section 33 social security — `07`.)

*90-day target: ~10 clients, **~฿39k/mo recurring** (10 × ฿3,900) + ongoing build income, system humming.*

---

## The numbers to watch
| Metric | Why | Healthy early sign |
|---|---|---|
| Leads contacted / day | Activity drives everything | 5–10 |
| Reply rate | Is the opener working? | warm audit ≫ cold blast |
| Audit → proposal → win | Your funnel | aim 20–35% proposal→win |
| **Monthly recurring revenue** | **The real business** | climbing every month |
| Churn | Are clients staying? | <1/quarter; reports fix this |

## The one-sentence strategy
**Dominate one town, deliver fast with AI, bundle the website + Google Maps, prove results, and let Thai referrals compound — while [girlfriend] owns every client relationship.**

## When you're ready to scale (post-90 days)
- Add segments (restaurants, spas, clinics, dive shops, tours) — same engine.
- Add a Thai VA, then a salesperson; you become the owner, not the doer.
- Consider a Thai limited company (and, if wanted, the boyfriend as ≤49% investor — done properly, `07`).
- Productise more (set monthly content, review-generation kits) to push margins higher.
