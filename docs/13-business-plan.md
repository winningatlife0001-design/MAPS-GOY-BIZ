## The model in one line
She sells + owns the relationships; **AI does the production**; costs stay tiny; **recurring monthly fees compound**. The only real constraint is her selling/relationship time (a VA is added as the client base grows).

**One-time build:** ฿15,000 (small) → ฿18,000 (hotel) → ฿25,000+ (bigger). *(~$420–$700; your "$500" sits right here.)*

## Assumptions (base case)
- **New paying clients/mo:** ramps 1 → ~5, then steady ~4–5 (solo + a VA). Year-1 = ~36 clients, Year-2 = ~50.
- **Churn:** 4%/mo (clients do leave; reports + owning hosting/Maps keep it low).
- **Costs:** AI tools/scrapers ฿3,500→7,000/mo; rank-tracking/SEO tools ฿2,500/mo (from month 5); **a Thai VA from ~20 clients (฿15,000/mo), more at ~45 clients**; ~฿50/client delivery; payment fees 1.5%.

## Why the recurring number is the real story
By month 24 (base) she has **~฿392,000/month coming in from retainers** before a single new build — that's **~฿4.7M/year of recurring revenue** that mostly repeats. That's the asset she's building. Builds are the on-ramp; retainers are the business.

## Startup capital needed: almost nothing
- Business registration + bank/PromptPay: ~฿1,000–5,000
- Tools month 1 (AI + scrapers): ~฿3,500
- A few domains, buffer: ~฿5,000
- **Total to start: ~฿10,000–20,000.** This is a time-and-effort business, not a capital one. It's **profitable from month 1** in the model (costs are far below revenue).

## Break-even & risk
- **Break-even is immediate** — there's no big upfront spend to recover.
- Main risks (full register in `docs/09`): slow sales start (→ conservative case, still ฿3.4M/2yr), churn (→ monthly reports), single-person bottleneck (→ hire the VA on schedule, keep systems tight), quality at volume (→ human-check all Thai/Chinese).

## Capacity & hiring path
- Solo to ~20 clients. Add a **Thai VA** (outreach + admin + posting) around month 9–10. A second VA / junior around ~45 clients. She stays on sales + relationships + quality; AI + VAs do the rest.

## What would push it to "optimistic"
Referrals (huge in Thailand), dominating one town then expanding, the **200% tax-deduction** urgency (until Dec 2027), and upsells (extra languages, photo packs, Google free-booking-links setup, seasonal promos).

## Bottom line
Even the **conservative** case clears **~฿3.4M (~$94k) profit over 2 years** with near-zero capital and one person + AI. Base case ~**฿5.7M (~$159k)**. The model is in the repo — change the assumptions (clients/mo, churn, prices) and the numbers update.
