# 09 — Competition, Risks & What People Miss ⚔️

## Who else is out there

| Competitor | What they offer | Price | Where they fall short for a small hotel |
|---|---|---|---|
| **MakeWebEasy** | Thai DIY builder, e-commerce focus | ฿4,500–12,500/yr | No hotel booking, no Maps help, fully self-serve |
| **ReadyPlanet** (R-Web) | Thai all-in-one (site, LINE OA, CRM) | ~฿9,500+/yr | Still DIY; not hospitality-specific; not hands-on |

| **Specialist hotel agencies** (Placeworks, The Percentage) | Great hospitality work | premium | High minimums — they ignore the micro 5–20 room places (our zone) |
| **OTA "property pages"** (Agoda/Booking) | A listing on their platform | "free" + 15–25% | Not owned; no brand control, no guest data, no direct bookings |

**Our gap to own:** the **micro 5–30 room** hotels/guesthouses (and small F&B/spas) that agencies ignore and DIY tools fail, delivered with **Thai-language human care + website + Google Maps bundled** — which almost nobody does. Compete on **value + service + speed + bundle**, *not* on being cheapest.

## Why so many still have no good site
DIY builders exist, yet small hotels still don't have decent sites because of **time, tech skill, language, and trust** — not because tools are unavailable. That gap is the whole business; a friendly local who "just handles it" wins.

## ⚠️ Risk register (and how we manage each)

| Risk | How real | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| **Client churn** (web/SEO churn ~38%/yr industry-wide) | High | Monthly **results report** tied to bookings (not vanity metrics); annual-prepay discount; quarterly check-in; own the hosting/Maps so leaving is a hassle |
| **Scope creep** (most agencies bleed ฿/mo to unpaid extras) | High | **Productised** packages; written deliverable list; "change-order" for anything extra; never offer "unlimited revisions" |

| **AI disclosure / trust** | Med | "AI-assisted, human-checked" — be matter-of-fact; quality silences the question |
| **Single-person bottleneck** (existential) | Very high | **Systematise from day 1** (these templates/SOPs); cap active clients to what one person can serve; line up a backup contractor for cover |

## 🎯 What people commonly MISS (learn these now)
1. **The money is the retainer, not the build.** The one-time fee is just the on-ramp; recurring care/Maps is the business. Structure it from day one.
2. **Bundle Google Maps from the start** — for many hotels it drives more bookings than the site, and nobody local does it systematically.
3. **Always include a booking path.** A pretty "brochure site" that leaves them 100% OTA-dependent is a failure.
4. **Own the infrastructure** (DNS/hosting/Maps management, with client consent) — it's standard and it kills churn.
5. **Sell to the real decision-maker, fast.** Small-hotel owners wear every hat; pitch in **60 seconds, specific, in Thai**, not a long deck.
6. **Don't skip discovery.** 10 minutes understanding their guests/competitors makes the site actually convert.
7. **Thai-language quality = trust in *everything*.** One clumsy Thai sentence makes them doubt the whole product. Native review, always.
8. **Collect proof from month one.** Testimonials + before/after results compound into referrals (the #1 growth channel in Thailand).
9. **Systematise before scaling.** Don't take 10 clients before the build/review/contract/payment flow is repeatable — or all 10 get mediocre work and you burn out.
10. **Define "done."** A written deliverable checklist + sign-off stops infinite requests.
