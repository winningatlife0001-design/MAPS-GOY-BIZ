# 14 — Feasibility Analysis (with real data & evidence) 📐

The hard-nosed "will this actually work?" doc. Every assumption below is tied to a benchmark and a source. Confidence: **H** = 3+ sources, **M** = 1–2, **L** = inferred. Where Thai-specific data doesn't exist, we say so and use global benchmarks **conservatively**.

> **Verdict up front:** Feasible. To hit the base-case (~฿5.7M profit / 2 yrs) she needs ~**3–4 new clients per month** and ~**70%+ annual retention**. The math says that requires ~**5–7 outreach contacts per working day** at normal close rates. That is a realistic workload for one person + AI. The biggest *risk* is not demand or cost — it's **consistency of outreach** and **retention**, both of which we engineer for.

---

## 1. The sales funnel — with benchmark conversion rates

How a contacted lead becomes a paying client. We use an **audit-led, warm, LINE-first** approach (not cold email blasting), which the evidence says converts far better.

| Funnel stage | Rate used | Evidence / benchmark |
|---|---|---|
| Lead → responds/engages | **20%** | Cold email reply averages ~8.5% (1–12% range) [Sopro/Saleshandy]; **audit-led outreach converts 3–5× baseline** [GetMapLeads]; Thai LINE/Facebook are warm channels with ~94% LINE open vs <15% email [DataReportal/Inspira]. 20% is mid — *higher than cold email because it's personalised + local + multi-touch.* **(M)** |
| Responds → real conversation / quote | **50%** | Standard SMB qualification step; many who reply are curious. **(M, inferred)** |
| Quote/proposal → **closed (paid)** | **30%** | Web-design agency proposal-to-paid on **vetted/warm niche lists runs 20–35%** [GetMapLeads/Gigradar]. Referrals close **38% faster** and are trusted by 92% [warm-referral research]. **(M)** |
| **Net: contacted → client** | **~3%** | 20% × 50% × 30%. i.e. **~3 clients per 100 contacts.** |

**Channel reality (Thailand):** cold *email alone* is weak (<15% open, distrusted). Cold **calling** to local businesses converts ~2.3% to a meeting across 200k+ calls [Martal] — fine as a *first touch* that moves to LINE. **LINE/Facebook + a free audit is the engine; email is for sending the quote.** [DataReportal, Inspira, b2bdataindex]

### What that means for her daily workload
| If close funnel is… | Clients per 100 contacts | Contacts needed for **4 clients/mo** | Per working day (20 days) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Conservative (2%) | 2 | 200 | **10/day** |
| **Base (3%)** | 3 | ~130 | **~6–7/day** |

→ **5–7 personalised contacts a day** gets her to the base case. Lead supply is a non-issue: one Koh Samui scan alone returned **339 no-website hotels + 24 Facebook-only** (real, this week). Finding leads costs ~฿150/1,000. **The bottleneck is her outreach consistency, not leads or money.**

## 3. Retention & churn — the make-or-break number

Recurring businesses live or die on churn. Evidence:
- Web/SEO **agency churn is reported ~38%/yr**; retainer agencies consider **>20%/yr "concerning"** [QuickSEO 2026, SEOMonitor]. **(M)**
- **Monthly value reporting + quarterly reviews improve retention 15–20%** [SEOMonitor]. **(M)**
- **Retaining a client costs 5–7× less than acquiring one; a 5% retention bump lifts profit 25–95%** [multiple]. **(H)**
- Thai culture is **relationship-loyal** — once trust (*bunkhun*) is built, clients rarely switch without reason [academic, ProQuest]. **(M)** This should push *our* churn **below** Western benchmarks.

**Our model uses 4%/mo churn (~38%/yr)** — deliberately *pessimistic* for a relationship-led Thai operator who sends monthly results and **owns the hosting/domain/Maps**. Lower churn (3%/mo) is the optimistic case.

| Monthly churn | ≈ Annual churn | Avg client lifetime | LTV per client* |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3% | ~31% | ~33 months | ~฿247k |
| **4% (base)** | ~38% | ~25 months | ~฿191k |
| 5% | ~46% | ~20 months | ~฿156k |

\*LTV = ฿16k setup + (฿7k/mo × lifetime). **Even the worst case = ~฿156k lifetime value per client** for a few hours of work + AI. CAC is mostly her time (near-zero cash), so **LTV:CAC is extremely favourable.**

## 4. Costs & break-even
- **Startup capital: ~฿10–20k** (registration, first-month tools, domains, buffer). No premises, no inventory, no big upfront.
- **Variable cost per client: tiny** (~฿50/mo delivery + ~฿400/yr domain). AI does production.
- **Break-even: month 1** in the model — monthly costs (฿5–48k as it scales) stay far below revenue.
- This is the key feasibility point: **there is almost nothing to lose financially.** Downside = her time. Upside = a ฿4–6M/yr recurring asset.

## 5. Sensitivity — what if it goes worse than planned?
The model's **Conservative** scenario already assumes a slow ramp (peaks ~3 clients/mo) **and** high 5% churn → still **~฿3.4M (≈$94k) profit over 2 years** and ~32 clients. For it to *fail* (lose money), she'd have to: spend real cash she isn't spending, get **near-zero** clients despite thousands of qualified leads, or never send a follow-up. The structure is hard to lose money on.

## 6. What has to be TRUE for this to work (the honest checklist)
1. **She does outreach consistently** — ~5–7 personalised contacts/day. *(This is the whole game.)*
2. **Close rate ≥ ~25%** on warm/audit-led proposals — in range per benchmarks, and helped by the free-audit hook + local trust.
3. **Retention ≥ ~70%/yr** — driven by monthly reports + owning the infrastructure + Thai relationship loyalty.
4. **Quality stays high** — every Thai/Chinese word human-checked; AI never auto-publishes.
5. **The legal structure stays clean** — Thai owner runs it; foreigner stays out (`docs/07`).

If those five hold, the base case is realistic. If only #1 is weak, she lands in the conservative case (still very profitable).

## 8. Bottom line (feasibility verdict)
- **Demand:** proven — tens of thousands of OTA-squeezed, no-/bad-website Thai businesses; 339 found in one Samui scan.
- **Unit economics:** outstanding — ~฿156–247k LTV per client, near-zero CAC and capital.
- **Required performance:** ~3–4 new clients/mo (≈6/day outreach) + ~70% retention — **within normal benchmarks**, not heroic.
- **Risk:** mainly execution (consistency + retention), not market or money. Worst modelled case still profits ~฿3.4M/2yr.

**This is feasible.** The plan's success rests on her doing steady daily outreach and keeping clients happy with monthly results — both of which the systems in this repo are built to make easy.
