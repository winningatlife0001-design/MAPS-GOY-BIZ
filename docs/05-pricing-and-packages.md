# 05 — Pricing & Packages 💰

All figures in **Thai Baht (THB)**. Researched against Thai agencies, Fastwork freelancers, and SaaS builders (June 2026). The strategy: **price below slow local agencies, far above the race-to-the-bottom freelancers, and anchor everything to OTA-commission savings.**

---

## The one number that sells everything: OTA commission

A small ~20-room hotel (฿3,000/night, 65% full) makes ~**฿1,170,000/month** in room revenue. If 80% comes through Agoda/Booking at ~20% commission, that's **฿175,000–230,000/month** handed to the OTAs — **~฿2 million+ a year.**

> Our entire pitch: *"Move even a few bookings a week to direct, and the website + our monthly fee pay for themselves many times over."* Recovering **one ฿3,000 direct booking per week ≈ ฿50,000–60,000/yr saved** — more than a year of our fees.

Thai owners feel this pain acutely. **Always quote our price next to their OTA bill, never in isolation.**

---

## Market reality (what others charge, THB)

| Thing | Freelancer (Fastwork) | Thai agency | Our position |
|---|---|---|---|
| 5–10 page site | ฿8,000–20,000 | ฿30,000–100,000 | **฿15,000–35,000** |
| Hotel site + booking form | ฿15,000–45,000 | ฿50,000–120,000+ | **฿20,000–40,000** |
| Monthly care/maintenance | ฿300–1,500/mo | ฿2,000–5,000/mo | **฿1,500–3,500/mo** |
| Local SEO / Google Maps | ฿3,000–10,000/mo | ฿8,000–35,000/mo | **฿4,000–9,000/mo** |
| One-time Google Maps setup | — | ฿1,500–5,000 | **฿3,500** (or bundled) |

**Affordability ceiling:** Thai SMB owners get resistant above **~฿5,000/mo** for ongoing services with fuzzy ROI. Keep each monthly line item under that and bundle smartly. A ฿15,000–35,000 build "feels attainable"; ฿50,000+ needs heavy ROI justification.

## Why this wins
- **Recurring revenue is the goal.** 10 clients on *Care + Maps* (฿3,900) = **฿39,000/mo (~฿468,000/yr) recurring** before any new build income. Retainers are where small agencies actually make money; a build is just the on-ramp.
- We undercut agencies on price **and** beat freelancers on service + ongoing care + Thai-language support + the Google Maps bundle (almost nobody bundles Maps).
- **Don't compete on price alone** — race-to-the-bottom clients churn and expect everything. Sell *value* (saved commission, more direct bookings, looking trustworthy to foreign guests).

---

## Simple sales math for the first year
- Goal: **10 build clients + 10 on Care+Maps** in ~6 months.
- Builds: 10 × ฿24,900 ≈ **฿249,000** one-time.
- Recurring by month 6: 10 × ฿3,900 = **฿39,000/mo** and growing.
- Costs are tiny: hosting ~free (Cloudflare Pages), domains ~฿400/yr each, lead scraping ~฿150/1,000, AI/tools do the production. **Margins are very high** — the scarce resource is [girlfriend]'s selling + relationship time, not money.
