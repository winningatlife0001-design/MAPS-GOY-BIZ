# 04 — Google Maps / Local SEO (our secret weapon) 📍

> The **step-by-step recipe** lives in `workflows/gbp-optimization-sop.md`. This doc is the *why* and *how we sell it*.

## Why Maps may matter more than the website
- **90%+ of travellers check Google Maps + reviews on a phone before booking.** A hotel with a weak/empty Google Business Profile (GBP) is invisible to them.
- Most small Thai hotels' GBP is **only 30–50% complete** — no proper category, few photos, no posts, unanswered reviews. That's low-hanging fruit we can fix fast.
- It's the **stickiest, highest-margin part of the business**: it's an ongoing monthly service (reviews keep coming, posts keep needing posting), almost no local competitor bundles it, and it produces a **visible monthly "we moved you up the map" report** that stops clients cancelling.

## What drives local ranking (so we sell the right things)
Approx weight of what we control (Whitespark 2026 expert survey):
- **GBP profile signals ~32%** — *primary category is the #1 lever.*
- **Reviews ~20%** — fastest-growing; **fresh review velocity now beats raw count.**
- On-page SEO ~15% · behaviour/engagement ~9% · links ~8% · citations ~6%.
- Plus **proximity** (where the searcher stands) which we can't change — so we win on relevance + prominence.

## The Maps product we sell
**One-time "Maps Rescue" (฿3,500, or bundled into a build):**
- Claim + verify the profile (video verification), pick the exact **primary category**, fill 100% of fields, set hours, list every amenity/service, upload 20+ real photos, fix **NAP consistency** everywhere, add the booking link, seed a review-request **QR code**.

**Monthly (inside "Care + Maps ฿3,900" / "Growth ฿7,900"):**
- Reply to all new reviews, post weekly updates, refresh photos, grow reviews (≥5 fresh/month, compliantly), run a **Local Falcon rank-grid scan**, and send a **1-page monthly report** ("#7 → #3 for 'hotel ao nang', +18 direction requests, +260 views").

## Reviews — the compliant way (protect the client!)
✅ Share Google review **short-link + QR code**, ask at checkout, polite LINE/email follow-up, NFC tap card.
🚫 **Never** pay/incentivise reviews, "gate" them (only asking happy guests), post fake ones, or use a shared review kiosk. Google removed **292M policy-breaking reviews in 2025**; the US FTC fines up to ~$53k *per* violation. We keep clients squeaky clean.

> Note: Google **retired the Q&A feature (Nov 2025)** — we focus on accurate profile + reviews + website content (Google's "Ask Maps" AI now answers from those).

## Hotels: turn on Google's FREE booking links
A verified GBP + a connected booking engine/channel manager (SiteMinder, Cloudbeds, WebRezPro…) lets the hotel's **own live rate appear on Google Search/Maps with an "Official site" badge — zero cost per click.** Direct bookings, no OTA cut. Premium upsell, real ROI.

## Suspension safety (don't get the client banned)
Suspensions jumped 80%+ (2023→24). We **never** keyword-stuff the business name, use fake/virtual addresses, create duplicates, or rapid-edit. Because we *also* build the client a real website, they always have a **Google-independent fallback** — a genuine selling point we mention.

## Tools (add as you grow)
- **Local Falcon** (~$25/mo) — the rank-grid heatmap that makes monthly reports look great.
- **GMB Everywhere / Pleper** (free–$15/mo) — see competitors' exact categories.
- **BrightLocal / Whitespark** ($33–90/mo) — citations + rank tracking + review requests.
Start manual; add tools after ~5 paying clients.
