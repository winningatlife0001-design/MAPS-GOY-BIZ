# 15 — The 20-Point Plan: make every outreach win 🎯

How we go from "a list of leads" to "she walks in already knowing the business and says exactly the right thing." Grouped by theme. Items marked 🤖 are things Claude/Apify does automatically.

## A. Deeper research BEFORE every outreach (so each message lands)
1. 🤖 **Reviews intelligence per HOT lead** — pull each lead's recent Google reviews → a 1-line *"what guests love"* + *"what's missing"*. The opener then references something specific & true. (Running this now on the top 40 Khao Yai leads.)
2. 🤖 **OTA-dependence check** — see if they're on Agoda/Booking → quantify their commission pain ("you're on 3 OTAs paying ~20% — that's ~฿X/month") in the pitch.
3. 🤖 **Facebook/social read** — followers, last post, whether they run ads → gauge digital sophistication + find a warm angle ("loved your songkran post").
4. 🤖 **Auto "gap list" per lead** — their exact weaknesses (no site / only N photos / no booking / no English) become the audit talking points.
5. 🤖 **Competitor snapshot** — who outranks them on Maps for "[area] resort" → "your neighbour is taking the bookings you could have."

## B. The pitch — exact, tailored, where it appears
6. **Tailor by situation × type × tier** — no-website vs Facebook-only vs bad-site; hotel vs spa; budget vs premium. (Built into **Studio** — it writes the message per business.) Keep refining templates with what actually gets replies.
7. **A 60–90s audit video** per HOT lead is the single biggest response booster — screen-record their Google listing, point out 2–3 fixes, end with a soft ask. (Where the pitch *appears*: the video + the LINE/FB message + the proposal.)
8. **Always open with ONE specific, true observation** — never a generic "I make websites."
9. **Multi-touch cadence**, tracked in Studio: FB/phone → move to LINE → 3 value-add follow-ups, spaced 3–5 days. Most yeses come on touch 2–4.
10. **Show, don't tell** — lead with the Smile House rebuild + demos + (soon) first real testimonials.

## C. Studio (her tool) — upgrades to build next
11. **Research panel auto-filled** by step A (reviews summary, OTA status, FB) so every lead opens "armed."
12. **"Generate audit script" button** — a ready voiceover for the 60-second video.
13. **Today's list + follow-up reminders** — next-action dates, a daily "6 to contact" queue.
14. **One-click export** back to a master sheet so status/notes are never lost (already in v1; add auto-backup).

## D. Delivery & quality (so we never get caught out)
15. **3-day productised build** per type/tier (templates are done) → same high quality every time.
16. **Native-check every Thai/Chinese word**; AI never auto-publishes. Quality = trust.
17. **Own hosting + domain + Google profile** for every client (with consent) → near-zero churn.

## E. Scale & money
18. **Dominate one area at a time** (Khao Yai now) → 2–3 reference clients → referrals do the selling (huge in Thailand).
19. **Track the funnel** (contacts → replies → proposals → wins) → fix the weakest step, not everything.
20. **Reinvest at ~10 clients**: hire a Thai VA for outreach/admin; she stays on relationships + quality; AI does production. That's how this scales without burning her out.
