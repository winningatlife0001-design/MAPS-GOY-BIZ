# 12 — Operator Playbook (her day-to-day) 🎯

The simple rhythm she runs every week. She does **sales + relationships + quality-check**; Claude does the production. (Thai version: `docs/TH/คู่มือ.md`.)

## The weekly rhythm
**Monday — fill the pipeline (1–2 hrs)**
- Ask Claude: *"Find no-website + bad-website [type] in [town]."* → ranked lead list.
- Pick the week's 🔥 targets; for the top ones ask Claude for a **free 2-min audit** to send.

**Tue–Fri — outreach (the #1 job, ~1 hr/day)**
- Contact **5–10 leads/day**: Facebook DM or phone → move to **LINE**. Lead with the free audit, never a hard pitch (`templates/outreach/`).
- Reply to anyone who's warm. Send proposals (`templates/proposals/`). Patience — 2–4 touches.

**Build days (as clients sign)**
- Collect intake + photos (`templates/client-intake/`) → hand to Claude → review the draft → get client's written "approved" → Claude deploys.
- Collect **50% deposit before starting**, balance before go-live.

**Friday — keep clients happy (anti-churn)**
- Claude prepares each client's **monthly Google-Maps report**; she sends it on LINE with a friendly note. This is what stops cancellations.

## Per-client monthly (Claude does, she reviews)
Reply to new reviews · 4 Google posts · refresh photos · rank scan + 1-page report · any small edits.

## Targets by phase
| Phase | Goal | Focus |
|---|---|---|
| Month 1 | 1–2 paying clients + 1 free demo to show | learn the loop, get proof |
| Months 2–3 | 4–10 clients | referrals, testimonials, faster builds |
| Months 4–6 | ~15–20 clients | push monthly plans, add Local Falcon |
| Months 7–12 | ~30 clients | hire a VA, systematise |
| Year 2 | 45–60 clients | VA(s) handle delivery; she sells + manages |

## KPIs to watch (keep a simple sheet)
- Leads contacted/day (5–10) · reply rate · proposals sent · **win rate** (aim 20–35%) · **active clients** · **monthly recurring revenue (the big one)** · churn (<1/quarter).

## Golden rules
1. **Recurring > one-off.** Always move a client onto a monthly plan.
2. **Own the hosting/domain/Maps** (with consent) — kills churn.
3. **Human-check every Thai/Chinese word** before sending — quality = trust.
4. **Never let a foreigner do the work or run it** (legal — `docs/07`). She's the genuine owner.
5. **One town at a time**, collect proof, let referrals compound.
