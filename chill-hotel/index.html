A building that gets out of the way, so the mountains don’t have to compete.

Chill Hotel was designed around the view — board-formed concrete, warm wood, and big panes of glass that frame the hills. Rooms are calm and uncluttered, with good beds, blackout curtains and proper coffee.

Downstairs there’s a café and a pool that catches the afternoon sun. Upstairs, quiet. That’s the whole idea.
