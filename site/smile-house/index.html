Nothing fancy — just clean, comfortable rooms by the water at a price that leaves money for the fun stuff.

Tharapana sits right on the creek, a short hop from the national park, vineyards and the night market. Rooms are bright and spotless, the pool is open all day, and there’s plenty of parking for the drive up from Bangkok.

Bring the family, bring friends, bring the dog — we’re that kind of place.
