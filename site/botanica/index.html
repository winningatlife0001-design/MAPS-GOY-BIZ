The Botanica Idea

A study in modern calm. Walls of glass open to the valley; private pools hold the sky. Here, luxury is not ornament — it is space, light, & the quiet of the mountains.

01 — The Place

Where nothing stands between you and the view

Set among the green folds of Khao Yai, Botanica is a collection of contemporary villas designed around a single idea. Floor-to-ceiling glass dissolves the line between room and forest. Each villa holds its own plunge pool — and its own square of sky.

The Experience

Four ways to slow down

Stay, dine, unwind and explore — all within the green quiet of the valley.

02 — Stay

Villas of glass & stone

Spacious, light-filled villas with private plunge pools, outdoor tubs, and balconies that open straight onto the mountains.

03 — Dine

A table with a view

Thai and international plates, an unhurried breakfast on the balcony, and golden-hour drinks high above the valley.

04 — Unwind

Water, warmth & quiet

Swim in the outdoor pool, soak in a hot tub beneath the stars, and let the forest set the pace of the day.

05 — Explore

Khao Yai, at your door

Vineyards, waterfalls and the national park — Thailand's first, a UNESCO site — lie minutes from the gate.
