One charcoal grill, recipes from the family’s home province, and a car park that fills up every weekend.

Krua Khao Yai has cooked the same honest Isan and central-Thai food for over fifteen years. Ribs smoked low over coconut husk, jungle curry loud with fresh peppercorn, whole river prawns split and grilled.

Big tables for groups, cold beer, and a long veranda that catches the evening air off the mountains. Come hungry.
