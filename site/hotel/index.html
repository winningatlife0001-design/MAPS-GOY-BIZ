Built for the kind of weekend where the only plan is no plan at all.

Hidden Village is a small family-run retreat — ten villas, set far enough apart that you’ll rarely see another guest. Each has its own pool or jacuzzi, an outdoor shower, and a terrace that looks straight into the hills.

Breakfast comes to your terrace. The pool is warm by mid-morning. The loudest thing you’ll hear is the cicadas.
