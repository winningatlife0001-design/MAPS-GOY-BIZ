The Hidden Village

Tucked down a quiet lane — a handful of villas, a great deal of green, & the feeling that the whole valley belongs to you.

01 — The Place

A village built for slowing down

A small collection of private villas in landscaped gardens beneath the Khao Yai range. Golf carts hum along the paths; mist settles over the lawns at dawn. The kind of place you book for two nights and leave wishing it were five.
