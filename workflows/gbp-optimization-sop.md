# Workflow 3 — Google Maps / Google Business Profile (GBP) Optimization 📍

For a small Thai hotel, **a great Google Maps listing often drives more bookings than the website itself.** 90%+ of travellers check Maps + reviews on their phone before booking. This is our highest-margin, stickiest upsell — and most local competitors don't do it systematically.

This is the SOP we run for every client (and the basis of the monthly retainer).

---

## How Google ranks local results (2025–2026)

Roughly: **Proximity** (where the searcher is — we can't change this) + **Relevance** (how well the profile matches the search) + **Prominence** (reviews, engagement, citations). We optimise Relevance + Prominence.

Approx weighting of the levers we *can* control (Whitespark 2026 expert survey):
- **GBP profile signals ~32%** (category is #1)
- **Reviews ~20%** (fastest-growing factor)
- On-page SEO ~15% · Behavioural/engagement ~9% · Links ~8% · Citations ~6%

---

## The checklist (ordered by impact — do top-down)

1. **Claim & verify the profile.** Unverified = no ranking power. New profiles now use **video verification** (record a continuous walk-through on the Maps app: exterior sign showing the name → street around it → inside → any license). Under 2 min, no edits, strong WiFi. Review ≈ up to 5 business days.
2. **Pick the exact PRIMARY category** — the single biggest controllable lever. e.g. `Hotel`, `Resort`, `Bed & breakfast`, `Boutique hotel`, `Hostel`, `Guest house`. Match the one true descriptor; copy what the top-ranked competitor uses.
3. **Set accurate hours** (incl. holiday/special hours). "Open now" at search time is a **top-5** ranking factor.
4. **Fill 100% of the profile** — description (≈750 chars, lead with natural keywords + town), phone, website URL, address, attributes.
5. **Add up to 10 secondary categories** (e.g. `Bar`, `Restaurant`, `Spa`, `Conference center`) to catch adjacent searches.
6. **List every service & amenity** — for hotels tick *every* box (free WiFi, pool, parking, airport shuttle, pet-friendly, A/C, family rooms, accessibility…). Google filters hotel searches by these; gaps = invisible in filtered results. Services with keyword-rich descriptions measurably help ranking.
7. **Upload 20+ real photos** — exterior (address visible), lobby, each room type, pool, dining, views. Real, recent photos beat stock. Refresh quarterly. **Don't bother geotagging** — studies show no reliable ranking benefit.
8. **Reviews — the engine of prominence** (see below). Aim for **≥5 fresh reviews/month** (velocity now beats raw count).
9. **Respond to every review** within 24–48h, politely, in the guest's language. Response rate is itself a signal.
10. **Post weekly** (offers, events, updates). <30% of hotels post regularly → easy edge.
11. **Add the booking/appointment URL** → point it at the client's new direct-booking site.
12. **NAP consistency** — identical Name/Address/Phone everywhere (Google, Facebook, TripAdvisor, Agoda, Booking, Apple/Bing Maps). Consistent NAP → ~23% more clicks. Even "St" vs "Street" mismatches hurt.
13. **(Hotels) Set up Google's FREE booking links** — verified GBP + a connectivity partner (channel manager/PMS like SiteMinder, WebRezPro, Cloudbeds) feeds live rates to Google so the hotel's *own* rate shows with an "Official site" badge, **zero cost per click**. Direct bookings, no OTA cut.

## Reviews: how to get more (the compliant way)

✅ **Allowed & effective:**
- Share the Google **review short-link** + a printed **QR code** (front desk, in-room card, checkout receipt, follow-up LINE/email).
- Ask in person at a happy moment (checkout); send a polite follow-up message.
- NFC "tap for review" card at reception.

🚫 **Never do this (suspension / fines):**
- Pay/incentivise reviews (discounts, gifts) — banned; FTC fines up to ~$53k/violation in the US, and Google removed **292M policy-violating reviews in 2025**.
- **Review gating** (only asking happy customers, screening out unhappy ones) — banned and actively enforced.
- Fake reviews from staff/family, or a shared "review kiosk" device (banned Apr 2026).
- Dictate wording or star count.

> Note: Google **discontinued the Q&A feature (Nov 2025)** — don't rely on seeding Q&A anymore. Keep the profile, website, and reviews accurate/keyword-rich instead (Google's "Ask Maps" AI now answers from those).

---

## Suspension risks — protect the client's listing

Suspensions surged 80%+ (2023→2024). Avoid:
- Keyword-stuffed business **name** (must be the real trading name only).
- Fake/virtual address, PO box, mismatched address vs website.
- Duplicate listings; rapid-fire edits to name/address/category.
- Lead-gen spam (profiles for areas you don't serve).

If suspended: use the in-dashboard **Appeals** tool; have storefront photos, business license, and a utility bill ready (you get a ~60-min window to upload evidence). **Because we also build the client a real website, they always have a Google-independent fallback** — a key selling point.

## Tools (optional, for scale)
- **Local Falcon** (~$25/mo) — geo-grid heatmap of where the hotel ranks across town. Great for *showing the client progress* in monthly reports.
- **GMB Everywhere / Pleper** (free–$15/mo) — spy on competitors' exact categories.
- **BrightLocal / Whitespark** ($33–90/mo) — citation building + rank tracking + review requests.

Start free (do it manually); add a tool once you have ~5+ paying clients.
